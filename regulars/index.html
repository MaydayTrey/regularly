What is Regularly?

Every business has regulars: the people who come back week after week. Regularly is an app that gives those regulars priority at the places they already go, and gives business owners one place to reach and reward all of them. It is under development and launching in Cincinnati and Dayton first.

For regulars

  • Your recurring slot, held for you.
  • First dibs when something opens up.
  • A direct line to the business. No rebooking from scratch.

For business owners

  • Every regular in one place.
  • Held slots for the people who keep you busy.
  • Reach and reward all your regulars at once.
